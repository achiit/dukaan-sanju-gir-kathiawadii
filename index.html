About us

A full plate, a fair price, and a warm welcome.

I grew up eating Kathiawadi food at family gatherings, where nobody ever left the table hungry and the elders kept serving until you folded your hands. That is the feeling I wanted to bring to Rah.

We opened our doors in 2026, just five months ago, right beside AR Petrol Pump on Jetda Road. The idea was simple: good, traditional food at a price every family and every traveller can afford.

Come hungry. We will keep serving. If you cannot make it in person, just message me on WhatsApp and we will sort it out.
